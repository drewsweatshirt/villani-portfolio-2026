Overview

Project Goal

The goal of this project was to create a manned FPV mars-like rover that can excavate payload in a sandpit and deposit said objects into a bucket for points. This must be completed controlled blindly, relying on the cameras and VR headset along with our controller.

Section 1

Initial Concept

We were given a set of rules from ASME regarding the IAM3D 2026 competition, as mentioned above in the "Project Goal" section. I independently designed, printed, and assembled the multi-part chassis system contining an inner and outer bracket, diagonal crossmembers pinned into the plates, and 3 plate layers for storage and reinforcement.

Section 2

Chassis Design

Our chassis began as a flat, non-rigid PLA plate and was further progressed into a symmetrical 4-plate design joining the inside plates with diagonal 45 degree hollow compression-focused crossmembers. All force flowed through the chassis into these crossmembers, and the ideal inner and outer diameters / specifications for the rods were calculated using formulas given in Mechanics of Solids (EAS209), all included in our 120+ page design report.
